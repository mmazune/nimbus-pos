# Look review 2: built screens vs drawings

Scoring: each point 0–2, in this order: Action · Controls · Cut/squeezed · Type · Surfaces · Colour · Spacing · Nav · Lists · Numbers · Words · Brand. A pass needs 20/24 or more with no zero.
Things the brief says were left out for lack of data are not scored as faults.

## 1 Main: 22/24, PASS
2·2·2·2·2·2·2·1·2·2·2·1
- The area chips are sorted A–Z, so "Bar" comes before "Main hall". The drawing leads with the selected or home area, Main hall.
- Search sheet: the single result is a small card on the left with empty space beside it. The card shows "0 min" where the drawing shows a figure, and the sheet sits flush against the bottom edge with no bottom padding.
- The logo tile is a blank white square. The drawing has the same, but the brand finish still looks unfinished.

## 3 Floor-1920: 21/24, PASS
2·2·2·1·2·2·2·2·1·2·2·1
- Tab tiles use a stacked layout: a small "Tab" label above a smaller number. Their numbers sit about 16px lower than every other tile, which breaks the grid's baseline rhythm. The drawing puts "Tab 1" inline at full number size.

- "Find order" in the corner is fine. The Late/Bill filters are left out, as allowed.

## 5 Reservations-Waiter: 21/24, PASS
2·2·1·2·2·2·2·2·1·2·1·2
- Phone list: the name column is so narrow that names break in the middle of a word or number ("17956867710 / 57"), and "No / table yet" wraps too. Each row becomes 4 lines. Names should be cut short with an ellipsis or wrap only between words, and the middle column needs more width.
- The phone sheet shows the guest's name twice: once as the sheet title and again as the heading right under it.
- Desktop matches the drawing well. The seated state is clear, with a green "Seated at Table 8".

## 6 Reservations-Supervisor: 21/24, PASS
