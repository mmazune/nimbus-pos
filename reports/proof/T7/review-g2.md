## Breaks use / wording
- No drawing has a hidden submit, a dead back or unreadable contrast. Every sheet has a close X and its main action is visible.

Order-Cancel says "8 lines" (there are 6 lines, 8 items). Split-Even preselects 4 for a 3-guest table. Cashier-Floor-Phone says 17 open but draws 12.
