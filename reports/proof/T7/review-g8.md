## Fix before pass

- **Manager-Menu-List (18):** draw "Find an item" as a standard 44 px or taller search field: rounded grey, padded, with the icon spaced from the text. Remove Blended Scotch and Lager from the All food view, or relabel the view as everything. Make the side group counts add up to 64, or add the missing groups. Add a "55 more" line or a pager under the table.

Should fix (these pass now):
- Manager-Today: make the Voids amount differ from Discounts, and keep "Close the day" visibly the secondary choice until the evening.
- Manager-Approvals: change "asked at 19:54" to 19:33.
- Manager-Person-Phone: show only "Edit" until something changes, then Save.
- Manager-Person: move Suspend into its own block at the foot of the left card.
- Settings-Stations: draw the times as fields, with amber and red colour dots.
- Settings-Areas: highlight its own side-list row.
- Settings-Business-Day: draw the yes/no settings as switches.
- Day-Close-Phone: put the choices in the next step instead of in the row text.
- Lists with no "N more" line: Staff, Staff-Phone and Menu-List-Phone.
- Approvals-Phone: keep names on one line.
