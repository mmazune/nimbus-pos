## Fix before pass

No drawing is under 20 and none has a zero. Report-Count-Differences passes at exactly 20. These number faults should still be fixed before build, because a report must show only what the records hold:

- **Report-Count-Differences (+Phone):** apply the stated limit consistently. Either mark Tonic, Beef mince and Cheese as over, or restate the rule (for example "both 2% and UGX 20,000"). Make last week's figures match the approved Waste report (−58,200, 3 over the limit), or change one of the two. Widen the Item and Difference columns so nothing wraps. Put the phone chips in time order.
- **Report-Voids:** add "Guest changed their mind 1 · 24,000" and "Kitchen ran out 1 · 8,000" to By reason so it adds up to 112,000. Make Table 12's chicken wings "Made, waste" to match Supervisor-Void. **Phone:** add the Chips row, or a "1 more" line.
- **Report-Sales-By-Item:** draw the share bars to scale (burger about 20%) and print the percentage beside each. Add an "N more items" line. Title the group card "Top 5 of 23 groups".
- **Report-Ticket-Times (+Phone):** make the hot kitchen's usual time fit its hourly rows (for example 14 min), and give the red times for every station.
- **Report-Cost-Margin / Report-Sales-By-Item:** use one Blended Scotch price on both. Fix the phone wording "costs No cost yet".
- **Report-Till:** move "Counted at close" out of the movements table. Do not label a shift that closed after midnight as "Today".
- **Report-Stock-On-Hand:** replace the range chips with an "as at" date.
- **All lists cut short** (Sales, Stock, Cost, Voids-Phone, Menu-List): end with an "N more" line or a pager.
- **Across reports:** "Today" ends at a different time on each (16:30, 19:42, 23:15, 00:34). Pick one "as at" time for the set. Stock on hand and Count differences light More, but the approved Waste report lights Stock with a "Stock › Reports" breadcrumb; pick one.
- **Wording:** "Request 0109" on Report-Day is a code; use the supplier or delivery name.
