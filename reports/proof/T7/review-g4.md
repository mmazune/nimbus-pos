## Fix before pass

None. Every drawing scores 20 or more and none has a zero. The lowest is 21 (Pay-Split-Part, Pay-Split-Part-Phone, Pay-Paid, Walk-Out-Phone).

Should fix (these pass, but are wrong or break use):
- **Pay-Mobile-Pending and -Phone (breaks use):** show "Record the payment" as disabled until a reference is typed.
- **Pay-Split-Part and -Phone:** show the amount on every paid part (mobile money 10,000). Use a different reference from the full-payment drawing. When Card is chosen, show the approval-code field as on Pay-Card, and change the button to "Record UGX 38,000 by card".
- **Pay-Mobile-Money and -Phone:** draw "They pay to" as plain text, not an input box. On the phone, put back the "check the message names Tapas and shows 98,000" line.
- **Pay-Card-Phone:** add the "Last 4 digits" field. On both sizes, flatten the steps card so it does not look tappable.
- **Pay-Paid:** make the change line plain green text, not a pill. Stop "20:54" wrapping on the receipt (both sizes). Rebalance the layout, for example by centring the success block and the receipt together.
- **Pay-Bill-Changed and -Phone:** make "Print the new bill" the filled action until it has been printed, or drop the "show the new bill first" hint. Make the two total cards the same height.
- **Refund-Supervisor-Phone:** show how the money goes back (mobile money or cash) and give a way back to the earlier step. Replace the "Same day … Later: the manager" line with plain words.
- **Walk-Out and -Phone:** change "the food stays out of stock" to "the food is not put back in stock". Rename or remove "Said they would come back" and "Paid, but nobody can find it" (they are not walk-outs). Fix the spaced hyphen and the "walk- / out" title break. Add the Note field on the phone.
- **Order-Hold-Fire-Phone:** put the "Going to the kitchen" label inside its card like "SENT". Shorten the toast to "Undo" plus the item name, or drop the heading.
