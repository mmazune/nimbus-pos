## Fix before pass

No drawing is under 20 and none has a zero. Owner-Branches-Phone passes at exactly 20. These should still be fixed before build:

- **Owner-Branches-Phone:** add chevrons to the branch rows. Bring back the per-branch flags (Till over 5,000, 6 voids). Add an "All 3 · 10,550,000" total. Label the Closed-yesterday card in UGX and give the closing times.
- **Owner-Branches:** title the page "Branches · Thu 26 Nov", not "Today".
- **Owner-Branch (+Phone):** make "Waiters 4 · 13 tables" agree with 17 open tables, or say what 13 counts. Drop the "Downtown:" prefix on a Downtown page. Say who is off in "Kitchen and bar".
- **Accountant-Purchases:** highlight only the open row (GV-2281). Give GV-2281 a short badge as well as its price badge, so that 3 short deliveries can be seen in the list. In the detail card, show ordered − short + price rises = invoice (481,800 − 19,200 + 12,000 = 474,600). Replace "35 more … below" with a pager or "Show 35 more".
- **Accountant-Purchases-Phone:** keep the amount on one line at the right. Add a "36 more" line and chevrons. Confirm whether the accountant phone bar has "Today", against the approved Accountant-Phone-Costs.
- **Accountant-Stock-Value:** align "no cost yet (10)" with Costs' "12", or explain the difference.
- **Accountant-Stock-Value-Phone:** show all 8 groups, or add "2 more · 1,250,000", so that the rows add up to 14,280,000. Decide whether Stock value is a main tab or under More, and match the laptop.
- **State-Detail-Error:** dim or disable the menu while the order cannot open. **Phone:** shorten the title so it does not orphan "open".
- **State-List-Loading:** make the skeleton rows 56 px, the same as the real rows. **State-List-Empty / Error:** keep the detail panel, or drop it in Loading too, so the layout does not jump. Cut "Showing nothing rather than an old list".
- **State-Detail-Loading:** add the footer button shape in the Order panel.
- **Offline-Held (+Phone):** give Held its own colour, distinct from Late. Advance the table times to 20:34 and mark table 7's 20:00 reservation as overdue. On the phone, label the top-right card figure (items or minutes).
